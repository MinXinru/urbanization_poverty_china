Fig. 4 | Relatively-poor-only poverty (RPO) status turnover and mover volumes are dominated by intra-sectoral status entrants and exiters (IS-entrants / IS-exiters) and status-persistent stayers (IS-stayers); cross-sectoral movers (CS-entrants / CS-exiters / CS-stayers) are typically several-fold to about an order of magnitude smaller across stock-weighted, gravity and demographic flow-composition approaches. Mean annual urban IS-entrants / IS-exiters under pooled schedules×scenarios are on the order of tens of millions of persons per year in 2000–2030 and rise toward tens–about one hundred million per year in 2030–2100 (panel f; ranges span schedules and SSPs). (a) Urban intra-sectoral turnover (urban IS-entrants, urban IS-exiters); (b) urban and rural IS-stayers; (c) rural intra-sectoral turnover (rural IS-entrants, rural IS-exiters); (d) movers with rural origin (CS-entrants, CS-exiters, CS-stayers); (e) movers with urban origin (CS-entrants, CS-exiters, CS-stayers); (f) mean annual volumes (million persons / year, log) for urban IS-entrants, urban IS-exiters, urban IS-stayers, rural-origin movers and urban-origin movers under three methods — Stock-weighted transition shares, Gravity-based transition model, and Demographic composition-based projection (short x-axis labels: stock-weighted / gravity / demographic) — pooled across 24 absolute×relative poverty-line schedules × BAU, LSD, HSD and HQD for windows 2000–2030 and 2030–2100 (mean across annual window endpoints, not interval sums). Markers at BAU: Focal (S23; star), LIC (S05; circle), LMI (S11; square) and UMI (S17; diamond). (a–e) use Method 1 (stock-weighted) trajectories for the focal 2010 Dibao-scaled × 50% median schedule (S23); HIST/HQD/BAU/LSD/HSD(Focal line) colours match Fig. 1. Abs.×rel. schedule envelopes (2.5–97.5%) for solid-line series are filled grey historically (excluding LMI/UMI schedules S07–S18) and filled blue under BAU after 2026; dashed- and dotted-line series show the same percentile band as thin outlines. Entrants / urban stayers solid; exiters / rural stayers dashed; status-persistent movers dotted. Directed origin–destination volumes average exit- and entry-side predictions for the 12 AP/RPO status-persistent stays and all AP↔RPO transitions; NP-linked flows remain single-sided. Dotted line: 2026.
