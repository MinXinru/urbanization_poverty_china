Fig. 3 | Linked-person transition trails and wave-annualised national flow volumes for absolute poverty (AP) and relatively-poor-only poverty (RPO), 2010–2022. Within each poverty block, columns pair national curves (left) with companion personal-line trails (right) for AP (left block) and RPO (right block); column headers mark AP and RPO. Rows (top to bottom): Total; urban intra-sectoral; rural intra-sectoral; cross-sectional (panels a–p). Trail panels plot CFPS individuals observed at the 2010 and 2022 endpoints: Urban/Rural on the left within each status band; non-poor (NP), RPO and AP coloured on the right. Urban and rural trails carry italic IS-entrants (left) and IS-exiters (right) callouts with IS-stayers and Intra-sectoral turnover labels; cross-sectional curves separate CS-entrants, CS-exiters and CS-stayers. Movers trails retain CS-entrants (left) and CS-exiters (right) labels; panel (p) additionally labels CS-stayers. Total trails (b, d) omit these labels; flow percentages are not shown. Curve panels report national volumes under stock-weighted transition shares (Method 1; million persons/year)—CFPS dual exit/entry rates applied to Lei schedule-specific six-state stocks over consecutive CFPS biennial windows, annualised by ÷2—not raw CFPS sample counts. Thick coloured lines mark the focal 2010 Dibao-scaled × 50% median schedule (S23); thin same-colour lines and shaded bands show other absolute×relative schedules (S01–S06, S19–S24; World Bank LMI/UMI schedules S07–S18 excluded), with the shaded schedule envelope spanning the 2.5th–97.5th percentile across those schedules. Legends appear on both AP and RPO curve columns. Points mark each window’s end year.
