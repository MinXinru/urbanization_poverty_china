Fig. 3 | Wave-annualised volumes of intra-sectoral turnover, stayers and movers for absolute poverty (AP) and relatively-poor-only poverty (RPO), 2010–2022. Columns: AP (left) and RPO (right). Rows: (a,b) national totals — Intra-sectoral turnover (IS-entrants + IS-exiters), intra-sectoral stayers, and cross-sectoral movers (CS-stayers + CS-exiters + CS-entrants); (c,d) urban — IS-entrants, IS-exiters and IS-stayers; (e,f) rural — IS-entrants, IS-exiters and IS-stayers; (g,h) cross-sectional — CS-stayers, CS-exiters and CS-entrants (national totals). Thick coloured curves: focal schedule S23 (2010 Dibao-scaled × 50% median). Thin same-colour curves and shaded bands: other absolute×relative schedules (S01–S06, S19–S24; LMI/UMI S07–S18 excluded), with the band marking the 2.5–97.5% envelope across schedules. Values are stock-weighted Method 1 annual volumes (million persons/year): CFPS dual exit/entry rates applied to Lei schedule-specific six-state stocks over consecutive CFPS biennial windows; 2-year window masses are annualised by ÷2 (same convention as Supplementary Fig. S12 / Fig. 4 historical windows). Points are plotted at the window end year. The six transition labels are IS-stayers, IS-exiters, IS-entrants, CS-stayers, CS-exiters and CS-entrants.
