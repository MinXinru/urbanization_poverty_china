Supplementary Table S1 | Holdout comparison of nested origin–destination gravity specifications. Indicators are the same-sector and same-status dummies 1_same (origin and destination share urban/rural sector and poverty status). In-sample R² is on log(F+1); holdout R² and MAE are on recovered flow levels F.

Notes. Ordinary least squares on log(F+1) with covariates z-scored on the training sample; predicted flows are recovered as F̂ = max(e^{ŷ} − 1, 0). Training windows are those with year1 ≤ 2018 (2010–2012, 2012–2014, 2014–2016, 2016–2018); holdout windows are 2018–2020 and 2020–2022 (metrics averaged). Geographic distance is omitted. Indicators denote the same-sector and same-status dummies 1_same that mark whether origin and destination share the urban/rural sector and the poverty status. Holdout R² on levels F favors the population-mass baseline (log N_o, log N_d, and 1_same), which is used for Approach 2 projections. Nested specifications that add income and Gini, or that drop the indicators, underperform on holdout. Extreme negative holdout R² values indicate catastrophic out-of-sample failure.
