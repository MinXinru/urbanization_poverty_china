Supplementary Fig. S16 | Sensitivity of relatively-poor-only (RPO) poverty-transition volumes to CFPS transition-share specifications under the stock-weighted approach and BAU. Panels show (a) urban intra-sectoral status exiters (IS-exiters); (b) urban intra-sectoral status entrants (IS-entrants); (c) rural intra-sectoral turnover, the sum of IS-exiters and IS-entrants; (d) urban intra-sectoral status-persistent stayers (IS-stayers); (e) rural IS-stayers; and (f) total cross-sectoral movers, the sum of cross-sectoral status exiters (CS-exiters), entrants (CS-entrants), and stayers (CS-stayers), attributed to the origin sector. For the exit side, pooled shares, 2020–2022 shares, or ±20% scalings of pooled non-stay shares are used while the entry side is held at pooled values; the entry-side variants apply the corresponding changes to entry shares while holding exit shares pooled. Future BAU six-state stocks are held fixed under the 2010 Dibao-scaled × 50% median schedule. Mean annual volumes are in million persons per year and are annualised by interval length on the Lei-calibrated scale. Near-term and late-century horizons are as defined in Supplementary Fig. S15. Pooled exit and entry variants coincide with the baseline by construction.
