Supplementary Fig. S15 | Sensitivity of relatively-poor-only (RPO) poverty-transition volumes to alternative post-2021 growth paths for the urban–rural subsistence ratio (RΩ), estimated with three flow-prediction approaches under BAU. Panels show (a) urban intra-sectoral status exiters (IS-exiters); (b) urban intra-sectoral status entrants (IS-entrants); (c) rural intra-sectoral turnover, the sum of IS-exiters and IS-entrants; (d) urban intra-sectoral status-persistent stayers (IS-stayers); (e) rural IS-stayers; and (f) total cross-sectoral movers, the sum of cross-sectoral status exiters (CS-exiters), entrants (CS-entrants), and stayers (CS-stayers), attributed to the origin sector. The three post-2021 RΩ paths (low, central, high) are crossed with stock-weighted transition shares, the origin–destination gravity model, and demographic cell-rate projection. Mean annual volumes are in million persons per year. Near-term means the BAU windows ending in 2027, 2028, and 2030; late-century means windows from 2030–2032 through 2098–2100. Flow volumes are annualised by interval length and expressed on the Lei-calibrated scale.
