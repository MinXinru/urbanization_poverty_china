Supplementary Fig. S17 | Sensitivity of projected relatively-poor-only (RPO) transition volumes to ±10% perturbations of the gravity-model slope coefficients. Coefficients on log origin and destination population masses and same-sector and same-status indicators are jointly multiplied by 0.9 or 1.1; intercepts, future BAU population stocks and margin calibration are held fixed. Values are mean annual millions of persons per year across BAU two-year windows ending in 2026, 2028 and 2030 (near-term), and windows from 2030–2032 through 2098–2100 (late-century). Panels show (a) urban IS-exiters, (b) urban IS-entrants, (c) rural intra-sectoral turnover (IS-entrants plus IS-exiters), (d) urban IS-stayers, (e) rural IS-stayers and (f) cross-sectoral movers comprising CS-exiters, CS-entrants and CS-stayers. For CS-stayers, the plotted volume averages origin- and destination-side annual predictions.
