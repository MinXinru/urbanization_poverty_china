Supplementary Fig. S14 | Absolute-poverty (AP) transition volumes and mover flows, 2000–2100. (a) Urban intra-sectoral turnover, comprising urban IS-entrants and IS-exiters. (b) Urban and rural intra-sectoral stayers (IS-stayers). (c) Rural intra-sectoral turnover, comprising rural IS-entrants and IS-exiters. (d) Movers with rural origin: rural-origin CS-entrants, CS-exiters and CS-stayers. (e) Movers with urban origin: urban-origin CS-entrants, CS-exiters and CS-stayers. (f) Mean annual volumes (million persons per year, log scale) for urban IS-entrants, urban IS-exiters, urban IS-stayers, rural-origin movers and urban-origin movers. Each mover series combines CS-entrants, CS-exiters and CS-stayers by origin. Panel (f) shows the Stock-weighted transition shares, Gravity-based transition model and Demographic composition-based projection (labelled Stock-weighted, Gravity and Demographic), pooled across 24 absolute×relative poverty-line schedules and BAU, LSD, HSD and HQD scenarios for 2000–2030 and 2030–2100; plotted values are means across annual window endpoints, not interval sums. Markers show BAU values for the focal 2010 Dibao-scaled × 50% median schedule (star), LIC $3.00 × 50% median (circle), LMI $4.20 × 50% median (square) and UMI $8.30 × 50% median (diamond). Panels (a–e) show stock-weighted trajectories for the focal schedule. Bands show the 2.5th–97.5th percentile schedule envelope: solid-line series are filled, while dashed- and dotted-line series use thin outlines. Historical envelopes are grey and exclude LMI and UMI schedules; BAU projection envelopes from 2026 are blue. The vertical dotted line marks 2026.
